FINA 402 · LECTURE 12 · 06 / 14

Macaulay duration: when does value arrive?

Two bonds mature in five years. One pays coupons along the way; the other pays everything at maturity. Do they deliver the value of your investment at the same time?

Macaulay duration is the average payment time, weighted by each cash flow’s share of the bond’s present value.

Coupon bond

Some value arrives earlier through coupons.

Zero-coupon bond

All value arrives at maturity: duration equals maturity.

Meaning of years

An average timing measure, not a promised payback date.

Modified duration: how much does the price move?

Dmod · price sensitivity

Convert Macaulay using annual YTM y and semiannual compounding.

P · current value

The bond or position’s current market value. ΔP is the estimated dollar change.

Δy · change in annual yield

New yield minus old yield, in decimals.
+1 percentage point = +0.01.

Connect to CAPM: sensitivity to a risk factor

Risk measureSensitivity to
Modified durationBond yield changes: negative price response
BetaMarket returns: systematic equity exposure
Higher expected market return → higher expected asset return
For positive beta and unchanged risk-free rate.
Higher yield (positive Δy) → lower price (negative ΔP)
For positive duration and unchanged cash flows.

Takeaways

Macaulay duration

PV-weighted average time. It is not a guaranteed payback date.

Modified duration

Approximate price sensitivity to a small yield change.

Bank equity

Leverage amplifies the gap between asset and liability sensitivities.

Low default risk does not mean low interest-rate risk. A need for cash can force a sale before maturity.
