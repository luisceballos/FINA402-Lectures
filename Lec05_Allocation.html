Part I · Expected return

What happens to return?

Expected return · B
The same weighted-average rule
E[rp] = wAE[rA] + wBE[rB]
= 1.20 × 10% − 0.20 × 5%
E[rp] = 11.00%

The spread matters

Relative to holding A alone:

ΔE[r] = 0.20 × (E[rA] − E[rB])

Here, the expected return rises by 1 percentage point. Reverse the expected-return ranking and the same trade reduces expected return.

Higher expected return here.
What happened to risk?
Part I · Volatility

Risk is not a weighted average

σp² = wA²σA² + wB²σB²
+ 2wAwBρσAσB
Use σA = 20%, σB = 15%, ρ = 0.30
= 1.20² × 20² + (−0.20)² × 15²
+ 2 × 1.20 × (−0.20) × 0.30 × 20 × 15
= 576 + 9 − 43.2 = 541.8
σp = √541.8 = 23.28%

Notice the signs

Both squared-weight terms are positive. The covariance term is negative here because one weight is negative and correlation is positive.

In this example, volatility rises from 20% to 23.28%. Leverage does not imply the same risk change for every possible pair of assets.
Percent units are used consistently throughout. Real portfolios also incur short-borrow fees, dividends owed, and trading costs.

Which point should we choose?

— Portfolio frontier— Best CAL★ Tangency
Slope of a capital allocation line

Choose the steepest attainable line

The tangency portfolio offers the highest expected excess return per unit of volatility.

It is the optimal risky mix. How much to combine with the risk-free asset is a separate decision.

Same two assets; now add rf = 3%. “Optimal” means maximum Sharpe under the model’s assumptions.

The story, in three decisions

From positions to an optimal portfolio

01

Allow long/short

Weights can be negative or exceed 100%. Gross and net exposure answer different questions.

02

Measure return & risk

Expected return is linear in weights. Volatility depends on squared weights and covariance.

03

Choose the risky mix

Maximum Sharpe identifies tangency. Risk preference determines the eventual mix with the risk-free asset.

If short selling is prohibited, could your optimal answer change?
Yes. If the unconstrained solution includes a short position, the feasible set changes and we must solve the constrained problem.
